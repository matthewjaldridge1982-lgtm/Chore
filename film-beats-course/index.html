Film Beats — Step by Step

SP-404MK2 + Moog Grandmother. Six weeks, two 45-minute sessions per week. Sample film audio, chop it into an instrument, build drums from things that aren't drums, and finish with a track built entirely from footage you're allowed to use.

How to use this course

Work through the weeks in order — each one assumes the pads and skills from the last. Every session has a short procedure to follow and a deliverable at the end (a short bounce, nothing more) — the point is to finish something small every session rather than collect samples forever.

Any underlined term or instruction — like or — opens a "Learn more" panel with a full definition or step-by-step instructions, sourced from the same manuals as the rest of the course. Confidence tags (verified, moderate, unverified) tell you how sure this course is about each specific instruction — see Failure modes & sources for what that means and what to double-check against your own unit.

New to the vocabulary entirely? Start with Part 0 — the vocabulary you need first before Week 1 — everything else assumes you've read it.
