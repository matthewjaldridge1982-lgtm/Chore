Week 6

Arrangement

Session A — structure

Step 1: Four patterns

Record four separate patterns into four pads:

  • Intro — texture and narration, no drums, 4–8 .
  • Main — everything, 8–16 bars.
  • Breakdown — drums out or heavily filtered, 4–8 bars.
  • Outro — elements dropping away, 4–8 bars.

Repeat the four times.

Step 2: Chain them

Learn and pattern-length editing on your firmware — the mechanics differ enough between versions that this course points you to the manual rather than a guess unverified for your version.

Step 3: Arrange around the voice

The narration is the spine. Find where the voice enters and where it lands, and build the structure to serve it: drop the drums under the important line, bring them in on the phrase after it. Don't write the beat first and drop dialogue on top — that's the arrangement that always sounds like two records playing at once.

Session B — perform it

  1. Set up the pattern chain.
  2. Rehearse three moves and no more: pad mutes, one filter sweep, one stutter.
  3. Record three complete takes.
  4. Keep the best one. Do not comp between takes — the constraint is the exercise.
Deliverable

A finished 2-minute piece. This is the end of the course — see Failure modes & sources for what to check if something didn't go as planned along the way.
