Week 5

Sources you can actually release

The legal position, stated plainly

Sampling a commercial film without a licence and releasing the result is copyright infringement in two directions: the film's soundtrack recording, and any underlying musical composition. This is settled law, not a grey area. What varies is enforcement, and the practical risk on a Bandcamp release with 40 listeners is low — but low risk is not permission, and it forecloses sync, streaming distribution and anything with a label.

Public Service Broadcasting license their archive material. moderate confidence — consistent with their public statements about working with archives, but specific agreements aren't verified here.

Clearable sources

  • Prelinger Archives (archive.org) — mid-century American industrial, educational, advertising and government film. The bulk of the collection is public domain and Prelinger has explicitly encouraged reuse. This is your richest seam for narration, period atmos and the specific tone of voice PSB works with. high confidence on the collection's general status; check the item page, because the collection is not uniformly PD.
  • NASA — NASA-produced material is generally not protected by copyright. Two caveats NASA states itself: NASA doesn't own third-party content that appears in its material (including licensed music in produced videos), and the NASA insignia and name can't be used in ways implying endorsement.
  • archive.org moving image collections more broadly — mixed licensing. Check the rights statement on each item. "It's on archive.org" is not a licence.
  • Australian archival (NFSA, Film Australia) — mostly licensed, not free. Assume you need permission unless a specific item says otherwise.

Session work

  1. Spend the first 20 minutes only on sourcing. Download or queue up 4–5 items.
  2. Find one piece of narration — a male or female voice with period delivery and room tone. This is the most valuable single element in this genre.
  3. Capture with , exactly as in Week 1.
  4. Build the whole piece from these sources only.
