Week 1

Get audio in, get it clean

Session A — capture

Step 1: Basic sampling (the manual method)

  1. Get into sample mode: press [EXIT] several times. If you're in pattern mode, press [PATTERN SELECT]. Pads should light orange.
  2. Press [REC]. Empty pads flash bright red; pads with samples glow dark red.
  3. Press [RECORD SETTING] to open the input settings screen. Set:
    • Input source — the input you're actually using (USB / LINE / EXT).
    • REC LEVEL — start low, play the loudest part of your film audio, raise until peaks sit around .
    • LENGTH — number of measures. Set this to 4 if you want the SP to stop automatically after 4 . Requires the tempo to be right first.
    • TEMPO — if you know the film cue's , enter it.
  4. Press a flashing pad to choose the destination. "WAIT" appears.
  5. Recording begins automatically when the SP detects audio verified. Start the film.
  6. Press the same pad again, or [REC], to stop. [EXIT] cancels.

Step 2: Skip-back sampling (the method you'll actually use)

This captures audio that has already passed through the input. You watch the film normally; when something good goes by, you grab it retrospectively. No pre-arming, no hitting record in time.

verified against the Roland reference manual

How it works mechanically: the SP continuously writes incoming audio to a circular buffer. Anything older than the buffer length (25 s default, 40 s with SBS Long) is erased. The buffer only runs when incoming audio exceeds the Auto Trig Level threshold.

  1. Confirm MARK Function is set to skip-back, not looper (SHIFT + [VALUE] toggles).
  2. Press [EXT SOURCE] to turn the external input on, and start the film playing.
  3. Watch the [MARK] button. It blinks when skip-back memory is recording. If [MARK] is not blinking, nothing is being captured. If it won't blink, your input level is below the Auto Trig Level threshold — raise the input, or lower the threshold in settings.
  4. When you hear something worth keeping, wait for it to finish, then press [MARK]. "SKIP BACK…" appears and the waveform of the buffer is drawn.
  5. Preview it: press [SUB PAD]. With [GATE] on, it plays only while you hold [SUB PAD] verified — Roland support article.
  6. Press [REC]. "Select Pad To Save" appears; empty pads blink red.
  7. Press a pad. The audio is now assigned.
Two ways to lose your capture, both permanent: pressing [EXIT] before assigning it to a pad, and powering the unit off. The skip-back buffer is volatile. Assign first, audition later.

Step 3: Today's captures

Get three things onto pads:

  • A 4-bar section of score ().
  • One line of dialogue — short, 2–4 seconds, with a distinctive voice or reverb.
  • One foley hit — a door, a footstep, a mechanical clunk. Something with a hard .

Step 4: Trim

  1. Select the pad.
  2. Press [START/END]. Adjust start and end points against the waveform.
  3. Pull the start point tight against the first transient — even 50 ms of silence at the front makes a sample feel late when you play it.
  4. Set the playback mode: on for the dialogue and the foley hit for now.

Session B — bank architecture

Step 1: Layout

Use one bank (A–J, selected with the [A/F]–[E/J] buttons). Fixed layout, every week from here:

PadsContents
1–4Drums
5–8Bass / low end
9–12Melodic
13–16Texture / dialogue

The point is muscle memory. In six weeks you should be reaching for pad 3 without looking when you want a snare.

Step 2: Record a pattern in real time

  1. Press [PATTERN SELECT].
  2. Press [REC]. Pads with no pattern blink red.
  3. Press a blinking pad — that's your destination pattern. The unit enters record standby and shows the RECORD SETTING screen verified.
  4. Set pattern length (in measures) and quantise. Turn the metronome and count-in on if you want them — both are SHIFT+pad shortcuts unverified which pads on your firmware; they're in the shortcut PDF.
  5. Press [REC] to start recording. Play the pads in time.
  6. Leave one part unquantised. Nudging one element off-grid is most of what makes this genre feel human.
Deliverable

60 seconds. Three samples maximum. No effects. Bounce it however you can and stop.
